Generated by `node scripts/economy-doc.mjs` from `env/economy-table.mjs` (read it in code as `ECONOMY` from `env/stats.mjs`).
Do not edit by hand: change the number, then re-run the script. A test fails when this file is stale.

Wheat is still called "Shards" inside the code. The game runs 24 ticks a second; every time below is in seconds.

## Start of a match
- 100 Wheat, 6 Crumbs, 1 Power Strip (2400 hp), 8 slices of toast in its stack.
- Crumbs gather on their own. On the play page (play/idle-crumbs.mjs, hooked in play/app.mjs) an idle Crumb goes to the nearest wheat in its cord reach at the first tick, and again 3 s after it stands idle. Right-clicking wheat still overrides it.

## Wheat
- A field tile holds 1800 Wheat (400 on the old GHT maps); a rich tile holds 2x.
- A Crumb takes 8.33 s to mine a load and carries 5 Wheat a trip (10 in a classic match). Walking time is on top.
- With six auto-gathering Crumbs on the home field, a Crumb brings about 28 Wheat a minute (about 167 for six), inside the 25-30 target, so the bank holds 400-700 after 120 s (tests/passive-income.test.mjs). The walk is short, so the mining time sets the pace.

## The bread chain
- **Mill:** grinds 10 Wheat into 1 flour every 20 s.
- **Well:** 10 Water a minute alone (one every 6 s) by the river or creek; a spring halves it. Each other Well of any owner closer than 8 tiles shares the draw: it adds (1 - d/8) to a sum and the Well draws at 10 / (1 + sum) a minute (two 2 tiles apart: 5.7 each). It must stand within 4 tiles of water and 6 tiles clear of enemy buildings.
- **Toaster:** every 5 s turns 1 flour + 1 water into 4 slices, if it is powered.
- **Pantry:** holds at most 6 flour and 6 water per side, plus 3 for each complete Toaster of that side.
- **Pilot light:** while the stack is under 4 slices, the Power Strip bakes 1 slice every 20 s by itself.
- A Crumb carries 2 slices to a fighter; the Strip keeps 2 slices back for emergencies.

Energy (env/energy.mjs) is what powers the kitchen. Each network adds up what it makes and uses:
- **Makes:** Power Strip 2, Generator 2, Windmill 3 (40 Wheat), Dam 6 (90 Wheat), Mill Dam 14 (160 Wheat).
- **Uses:** each Toaster 2; each fence 1 for every 8 tiles, rounded up.
- **Brown-out:** when a network uses more than it makes, fences go dark first (last one first), then the newest Toaster. A Toaster on dark power does not bake.

## The math, per minute
- A **Crumb** costs 50 Wheat.
- A busy **Mill** eats 30 Wheat and makes 3 flour (worth 12 slices).
- A busy **Toaster** uses 12 flour and 12 water and makes 48 slices. It outruns a single Mill: 4 Mills keep one Toaster busy.
- One slice costs 2.5 Wheat (10 Wheat per flour, 4 slices per flour; water is free).
- A fighting **Popper** burns 13.3 slices a minute, which is 33.3 Wheat of toast a minute, plus 3.75 Wheat upkeep. One Popper in a fight eats the output of 1.1 Mills.

## The target curve (what a match should feel like)
- **0:00-2:00, farm and kitchen.** Crumbs go to the wheat; first Mill, Well and Toaster (225 Wheat) by about 1:30. Six Crumbs bring about 150-180 Wheat a minute, so the first kitchen is paid for in about 1.5 minutes; flour and water are the real limit.
- **2:00-5:00, first squad.** Assembly Line, then 4-6 Poppers (75 Wheat each) and a few more Crumbs. Toast, not Wheat, decides how many shots the squad has.
- **5:00+, push with boxes.** Fences and stations carry the squad forward; a second Mill per 3 Crumbs keeps the toast coming.
- **12-16 min, a typical siege.** The Power Strip has 2400 hp, so a fed siege takes minutes, not seconds.

## Why these numbers (value-based prices and upkeep)
- **One Mill per 3 Crumbs.** A Mill grinds 10 Wheat every 20 s: 30 Wheat in and 3 flour (12 slices) out per minute. That is about a third of what 3 Crumbs bring (about 84 a minute), so two Mills keep six Crumbs' wheat moving.
- **Prices follow value.** Roughly sqrt(damage per second x hit points), then a bonus for range, splash or not needing toast: Popper 75, Zapper 65 (cheaper: it is fragile and burns toast fastest), Four-Slice 225, Broiler 175, Flak Popper 70 (nothing flies yet), Bagel 125 (needs power, no slices). Crumb stays 50.
- **Army limit: lower upkeep, no supply cap.** Upkeep is 5% of cost a minute. The other option, Supply = toast capacity, would need a new rule, a new HUD readout and new validation; the hard cap of 40 units already stops an endless swarm. One system, shown in the top bar's Upkeep per minute.
