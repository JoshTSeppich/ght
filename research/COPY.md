# Toastinator tooltip copy

Button format: `{name} — {cost} Wheat · [{hotkey}] · {line}`. Resolve cost and hotkey from the live card and ECONOMY table; omit fields for non-buildable objects. Never invent a shortcut or freeze balance numbers in prose. These are copy assets for HUD integration, not live HUD wiring.

## Current units, buildings and stations

- **Crumb** — Wheat in, supplies out; this little toaster carries the entire operation.
- **Popper** — One slice, one shot; keep this front-line professional fed and plugged in.
- **Zapper** — Short-range hot bolts; close the gap before announcing breakfast.
- **Four-Slice** — A heavy four-slice volley; brace for a substantial serving.
- **Broiler** — Lob bread shells with splash from a safe distance; the door is not armour.
- **Flak Popper** — Crouton bursts for airborne targets and tight swarms; clear the breakfast airspace.
- **Bagel** — Roll into the enemy; power required, slices respectfully declined.
- **Power Strip** — Home, Crumb training and emergency toast; lose this and breakfast is over.
- **Assembly Line** — Queue fighting toasters here; discipline comes off the belt.
- **Heat Lamp** — Fixed defence that fires at nearby enemies; hostile guests will be warmed.
- **Mill** — Grind wheat into flour; empty wheat stores mean an unscheduled break.
- **Well** — Draw water near a river for faster filling; dry ground is not a beverage.
- **Toaster** — Turn flour and water into ammunition; keep the power and deliveries coming.
- **Extension Box** — Extend power reach; link boxes and use Fence to secure the line.
- **Relay Pylon** — Carry power above rivers and cliffs; leave the ground lanes open.
- **Windmill** — Generate power on a high-ground pad; requisition the breeze.
- **Dam** — Generate power at a river site; put the current to work.

## Status icons (picker order, left to right, top row first)

- **Missing wheat** — No wheat; send a Crumb to the fields.
- **Missing water** — No water; check the Well and its deliveries.
- **No power** — No power; reconnect to the Strip.
- **Full pantry** — Pantry full; move supplies to a Toaster.
- **Hungry** — Hungry; deliver toast so this soldier can fire.
- **Pilot light** — Emergency toast below the reserve threshold; breakfast is still in service.

## First-match tutorial

1. Select a Crumb and right-click wheat; breakfast begins with field duty.
2. Build a Mill, a Well near water and a powered Toaster; wheat becomes flour, then toast.
3. Build an Assembly Line and train Poppers; keep toast arriving for their ammunition.
4. Place linked Extension Boxes to extend power toward the enemy; keep the breakfast cord connected.
5. Select your fed squad and attack the enemy Power Strip; disconnect their breakfast, protect your own.
