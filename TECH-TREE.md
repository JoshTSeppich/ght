## The bread chain, in plain words
Wheat is the money. Crumbs mine it from the fields and carry it to the kitchen.
- **Mill** (A): grinds 10 Wheat into 1 flour every 20 s.
- **Well** (S): draws 1 water every 6 s, if it stands within 4 tiles of water. At a spring it draws one every 12 s. Wells within 8 tiles of each other share the draw.
- **Toaster** (D): every 5 s turns 1 flour + 1 water into 4 slices of toast, but only while it has power.
- **Power** comes from the Power Strip, Windmills (C) and Dams (V). A Toaster on dark power does not bake.
- **Toast** (slices) is the ammo: a fighter needs slices to fire, and Crumbs carry them out to the fighters.
- **Pantry:** holds 6 flour and 6 water per side, plus 3 more for each complete Toaster of that side.

So: Wheat -> Mill -> Flour; Well -> Water; Flour + Water + Power -> Toaster -> Toast -> fighters.
